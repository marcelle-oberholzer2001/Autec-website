Satellite Exploration & Mine Planning

We use advanced satellite scanning and geospatial analysis to identify areas of mineral potential and support informed exploration planning. The resulting data helps define orebody targets, provides accurate GPS coordinates for priority locations and assists with the development of a detailed mine plan. This allows clients to focus their on-site exploration activities more effectively and reduce unnecessary time and cost.

02

Processing Plant Assessment & Custom Design

Every orebody and mining operation has different processing requirements. SA Autec assesses the client’s ore characteristics, expected production capacity, recovery objectives, site conditions and available infrastructure before designing a purpose-built mineral processing plant. Each solution is engineered specifically for the project, ensuring an efficient process flow, appropriate equipment selection and a plant configuration suited to the client’s operational needs.
