Overview

Reliable reagent handling for consistent metallurgy.

Autec chemical dosing systems provide accurate, dependable delivery of the process reagents and chemicals used throughout gold and mineral processing operations. Each system is configured around the specific reagents, dosing rates and plant layout of the application.

Typical duties include the make-up and dosing of reagents such as cyanide, lime, flocculant and activated carbon, supporting stable metallurgical performance and safe, controlled chemical handling across the circuit.

Key Features

  • Configured for the specific reagents and dosing rates of each plant
  • Reagent make-up and holding tanks with agitation where required
  • Dosing pumps sized to the required flow and pressure
  • Manual or automated flow control and monitoring options
  • Corrosion-resistant materials suited to aggressive reagents
  • Robust construction for continuous plant duty

Typical Applications

  • Carbon-in-Leach (CIL) and Carbon-in-Pulp (CIP) circuits
  • Leaching and conditioning tanks
  • Cyanide, lime and flocculant dosing
  • Reagent make-up and distribution
  • New plant installations and existing plant upgrades

Engineered Around Your Reagents.

Autec designs and manufactures chemical dosing systems tailored to each operation's reagent suite, throughput and control requirements — delivering accurate, dependable chemical handling for safe operation and reliable plant performance.
