Our Story

Engineering solutions built for African conditions.

SA Autec Industries was founded with a clear purpose: to deliver mining and mineral processing equipment that actually works in the African environment. Too often, off-the-shelf international solutions arrive on African mine sites ill-suited to local conditions, logistical realities, and operational budgets.

We design and manufacture our equipment locally, drawing on hands-on experience in South African and broader African gold processing operations. Every agitator, boiler, pump, and plant we build is engineered for the specific duty it will face, from remote alluvial operations to large-scale CIL processing facilities.

Our team combines deep metallurgical process knowledge with practical fabrication and commissioning experience, ensuring every solution we deliver is fit for purpose and backed by real support.

The Team

The people behind Autec.

Co-Founder & Managing Director

Chris van Rensburg

Co-Founder & Technical Director

A mechanical engineer with approximately ten years of hands-on mining experience in Zimbabwe, Chris is the technical driving force behind SA Autec’s containerised processing technology. He leads engineering design, plant layouts, mechanical development, process optimisation, research and development, and commissioning support, ensuring every solution is practical, efficient and built for real mining conditions.

Colin Chaga

Exploration Lead — Mozambique & Zimbabwe

With more than 25 years of geology and mining experience across Africa, Australia and the Americas, Colin leads SA Autec’s satellite exploration and mine planning services in Mozambique and Zimbabwe, bringing seasoned geological insight to early-stage exploration and practical mine development.
